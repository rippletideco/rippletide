The workspace's agents and integrations overview helps you decide which connection needs attention next. Check the selected workspace before interpreting its totals.

| Measure | What it means | Useful next step |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Human interventions | Traces involving a recorded human review or approval over the last 30 days | Inspect the recurring situations and their underlying traces |
| Risky actions | Calls to actions classified as high risk over the last 30 days | Inspect the action, evidence and existing Rules |
| Needs attention | Incomplete setup or missing active Rules | Finish runtime verification or review appropriate Rules |

Pending risk analysis and unavailable activity are shown explicitly. They are not zero risk or zero activity. A high-risk label is classification guidance, not proof that a call was unauthorized or blocked.

Select an agent to inspect **Context & actions**, **Business context**, **Rules**, and **Traces**. A declared inventory and a verified runtime are different states; use the connection's actual evidence requirements.

Recorded human interventions and Rippletide Rule activations measure different things. A trace may contain both. Rule match totals also count individual matching decisions, not simply one count per trace.

Use the fleet to choose a concrete case, inspect the exact evidence, propose a Rule, and verify it in Observe before enabling Enforce. For collaboration and access, see [Workspaces](/docs/workspaces).
